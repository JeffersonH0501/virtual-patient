Use multimodal evidence only as complementary information about observable behavior in the full verbal interaction. Do not infer interest, empathy, attention, emotion, understanding, friendliness, concern, warmth or other psychological states from a single cue. Missing or unavailable multimodal evidence must not be penalized. Mixed unclassified patterns are descriptive and have no inherent negative meaning.
Patient-turn observations describe the DOCTOR while listening, never the patient's face or voice. Doctor-turn speech observations describe the doctor's delivery only.
Smile behavior describes detected smile activity and activation only. Do not equate smiling with interest or empathy or infer emotions from it. No cue on its own establishes communication quality.
